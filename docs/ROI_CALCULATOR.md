## 💡 Custom ROI Calculation

### Formula: Your Organization

```
Annual Development Cost (Traditional) = 
    Features/Year × Hours/Feature × Developer_Rate

Annual Development Cost (AI Platform) = 
    Features/Year × 8 hours × Developer_Rate × 0.1 (QA overhead)

Annual Savings = 
    Traditional_Cost - AI_Platform_Cost

ROI = 
    (Annual_Savings × Years - Total_Investment) / Total_Investment × 100%

Payback Period (months) = 
    Total_Investment / (Annual_Savings / 12)
```

## 📝 Assumptions & Notes

### Calculation Assumptions

1. **Developer Rate:** $80/hour fully loaded (industry average for senior developers)
2. **Feature Complexity:** Medium complexity (2-4 weeks traditional delivery)
3. **Team Efficiency:** 70% productive time (accounting for meetings, overhead)
4. **Test Coverage:** 88% achievable with AI platform (industry best practice)
5. **Compliance:** PCI DSS, GDPR, SOX requirements (FinTech standard)
6. **Risk Values:** Based on Ponemon Institute data breach cost report
7. **Growth Rate:** 5% annual increase in savings due to efficiency improvements

### Exclusions

- Hardware/infrastructure costs (assumed existing cloud infrastructure)
- Training costs (included in Year 0 implementation)
- Third-party licensing (calculated separately)
- Opportunity cost of delayed features (not quantified)
